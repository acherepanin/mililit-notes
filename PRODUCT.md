# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Notes is used by authenticated users who need a reliable daily workspace for creating, organizing, finding, and sharing notes. The primary context is focused work: writing, editing, retrieving structured information, managing attachments, using data fields, and returning to important notes quickly.

Administrators use the same product surface to manage users, integrations, activity, statistics, and AI provider or bot settings. These admin workflows need density and control without feeling like a separate technical console.

## Product Purpose

Notes exists to be a trustworthy note workspace with an editor, hierarchical organization, admin controls, public share links, attachments, and AI-assisted workflows. Success means users can open the app every day, understand where they are, write or retrieve information quickly, and trust that sensitive actions, settings, and AI capabilities are clearly controlled.

The product should sell confidence and quality. It should feel like a polished tool people would pay for, not another generic AI startup surface.

## Brand Personality

Calm, modern, clear.

The interface should feel reliable, premium, and alive enough to be pleasant to open. It should be understandable for a broad audience, not tuned only for technical users. Working screens should stay quiet and task-focused; public marketing, pricing, and landing surfaces can carry more emotion, motion, and conversion energy.

## Anti-references

Avoid dry corporate admin templates that make the product feel generic or under-designed.

Avoid command-center aesthetics for everyday note work: excessive density, terminal cues, aggressive contrast, dashboard-first composition, and controls that feel built only for operators.

Avoid narrow personal-notebook tropes that make the app feel small, hobbyist, or overly soft.

Avoid generic AI/SaaS cliches: purple gradient branding, decorative orbs, neon glow, "Powered by AI" framing, pseudo-chat as the default screen, and visuals that read as a 2024 AI startup template.

Avoid decoration that competes with writing, retrieval, settings, or admin tasks. AI should appear as a useful capability inside the product, not as the entire identity.

## Design Principles

Trust is the first impression. Core surfaces should make state, ownership, permissions, and consequences visible before asking users to act.

The task stays in front. Editor, tree, search, settings, admin, and AI flows should prioritize reading, writing, scanning, and decision speed over decorative structure.

Polish earns attention. Motion, color, depth, and microinteractions should make loading, success, transitions, and control states feel finished without creating constant movement.

One product, different energy levels. Marketing and public pages can be bolder and more emotional; authenticated workspaces should preserve calm, clarity, and stable control placement.

AI is a capability, not the brand. AI features should feel integrated, permission-aware, and useful, with clear limits and recoverable actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for readable contrast, visible focus states, labeled controls, keyboard access, and clear interactive states.

Support reduced motion with `prefers-reduced-motion`: decorative motion should be disabled or simplified, while essential state changes remain understandable.

Use explicit labels and tooltips for icon-only controls. Avoid relying on color alone for status, warnings, permissions, or destructive actions.

Keep RU/EN localization in mind when designing layout, labels, and control widths. Text must survive longer Russian strings without clipping meaningful content.
